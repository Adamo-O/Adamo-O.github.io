Built and operate a Discord voice bot that plays a personalized
theme song whenever a user enters a voice channel, with configurable
outros, playback duration, and rotating song cycles.

<br />

The bot supports more than 1,500 saved profiles across 240 Discord servers.

<br />

It runs as a Dockerized Python service on Railway, with MongoDB persistence
and a maintained FFmpeg, yt-dlp, and proof-of-origin playback pipeline.
